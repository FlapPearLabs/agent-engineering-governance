## Detector contracts

The unit is a registered task × arm × repeat, with a paired task/base across arms, fixed order/randomization and seeds registered before runs. Preserve all attempts, failures and raw logs; do not select only green runs. Inputs and outputs are bound to task, arm, material SHA, base, candidate and gate configuration hashes in a protected manifest. Missing or ambiguous evidence is UNKNOWN, never PASS.

## M4 blind protocol and P4

One generator produces all three arms' review bundles from the same schema and identical field presence, including empty fields; neutral random IDs map back only in a sealed mapping inaccessible to reviewers. Remove arm names, case IDs, base SHA, identifying paths, treatment markers and injection metadata; normalize content without removing evidence needed to judge correctness. Check schema and field presence bytewise across all arms, scan structured fields and free text for markers and metadata, and test adversarial identifiers and absent/extra fields. An independent reviewer examines sampled bundles and records blind receipt before verdicts. If task content or the defect itself reveals the arm, record leakage and mark `BLIND_EVALUATION = VOID`; no claim of effective blinding from projection alone. Adjudication uses the same blinded evidence, with disagreements recorded; unblinding occurs only after signed verdicts. Exact-SHA review for promotion is separate and still required.

## Decision rules — proposed precedence, one result only

Before classification, require valid P1–P7, frozen thresholds and complete task/arm pairing, N and sufficient power; see §4 of the protocol for promotion. Invalid or missing data and unresolved high-value boundaries default to `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. A **verified** control escape can be reported even if other cells are incomplete, provided the affected control and complete acceptance procedure are valid; incomplete evidence cannot manufacture an escape.

1. `MECHANICAL_ACCEPTANCE_HAS_NO_TEETH`: any valid INJECT cell has verified retained F-010 and COMPLETE_GREEN (M2/M3), even if another adverse finding exists. Preserve all accompanying findings.
2. `PROCESS_ABLATION_UNSAFE`: no rule 1; valid paired evidence establishes an additional high-value MIN miss (M4), or a verified injected defect was not caught without COMPLETE_GREEN and the pre-registered failure attribution establishes a real mechanical miss. An unrelated gate failure alone is not a miss.
3. `PROCESS_ABLATION_SAFE`: no rule 1 or 2; all valid injection controls caught by the designated gate, no additional high-value MIN miss or false PASS (M4/M5), complete valid paired M2 results meet the pre-registered non-inferiority threshold and power, and observed M1/M6 and uncertainty are reported. This is limited to the tested population and defect class, not proof of general safety.
4. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: every other case, including missing thresholds/power, incomplete valid evidence or ambiguous findings.

The non-inferiority margin, test, confidence level, allocation, dependence model and sample-size calculation are **UNDECIDED**, and must be registered before any formal data. M1 and M6 describe process and interruptions, not independent proof of safety; M5 is an adverse signal and cannot by itself be converted into a pre-specified high-value miss without adjudication. These rules refine, not supersede, protocol §2.5; owner approval is required.

## Validity, stopping and cost

Minimum **N_PER_ARM ≥ 2** valid mechanically verifiable end states is a promotion floor, not a power guarantee; §2.2 separately requires ≥4 distinct qualifying historical tasks including t01. Freeze equal allocations and repeats after a power calculation, account for task clustering and missingness, and never silently replace failed or invalid cells. Report attempted, valid, invalid and unknown counts separately, including control t01's non-transitioning oracle. Preregister any rerun policy and distinguish reruns from independent samples.

Do not start unless P1–P7, task count, carrier/readback, complete controlled gates, blinded review, frozen plan, durable evidence location and budget are approved. Stop scheduling on a safety/authority breach, verified defect escape, broken blinding, loss of evidence integrity or gate controls, budget cap, or any stop required by protocol; preserve partial evidence and classify it by the rules above. Stop for review on repeated invalidity or infrastructure failure, using thresholds fixed before execution. No automatic inference from early stopping; pre-register any sequential analysis and multiplicity.
